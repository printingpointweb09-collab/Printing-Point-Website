1.  Executive Direction

The current website has the basic ingredients---corporate gifting,
printing services, products, company information, client proof and
enquiry CTAs---but the product experience is too shallow for a B2B
purchase decision. The redesign should therefore be architectural, not
cosmetic.

17. Visual / UX Rules

Keep the premium navy / gold / white brand direction, but use gold as an
accent rather than filling the interface.

Do not let decorative elements compete with product images.

Use one fixed product-image frame across the catalogue.

Do not crop products to fit the frame.

Use high-resolution product images with consistent internal padding
where possible.

Keep product cards equal in height within a grid.

Use whitespace and typography to create the premium feel rather than
excessive borders.

Use consistent CTA language: Get a Quote / View Details / WhatsApp.

Design mobile-first for product grids, filters and quote forms.

Avoid AI-generated generic product imagery when an actual
catalogue/product image exists.

18. What Must NOT Be Done

Do not simply add more products to the existing cards.

Do not create a different product-card design for every category.

Do not force identical specification fields onto every category.

Do not publish unverified MOQ, price, lead time or material information.

Do not copy competitor wording or visual design.

Do not overload the homepage with the entire catalogue.

Do not make users enquire just to discover basic product information.

20. Product-by-Product Update Workflow

Select one category.

Create/clean the master product data for that category.

Prepare product images in the standard image frame.

Write short + detailed descriptions.

Fill category-relevant specifications.

Add branding/customization/MOQ/packaging information where verified.

Upload into the reusable product template.

Check desktop + mobile presentation.

Move to the next product in the same category.

After the category is complete, review the category page as a
buyer---not as the website editor.
