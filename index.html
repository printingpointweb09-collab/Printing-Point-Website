Who We Are

Built for Corporate Scale

Founded in 1996, Printing Point has grown into a trusted name, known for precision, creativity, and reliability. With decades of expertise, we proudly serve individuals, businesses, and corporate clients with solutions that make every impression last.

"At Printing Point, printing is more than a service — it's our passion, tradition, and promise of perfection."

From business cards to banners, merchandise to corporate gifts, we bring ideas to life with precision and creativity. Our legacy is built on innovation, craftsmanship, and a commitment to excellence that has earned us long-lasting relationships with clients across industries.

Procurement FAQs

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 50 units across all orders, for both corporate gifts and commercial printing.

Can we review samples before bulk production?

Yes! We provide complimentary digital visual mockups within 4 hours. Physical pre-production samples are also arranged for verified corporate bulk orders.

What are typical delivery timelines?

Standard turnaround is 5-7 business days following digital proof approval. Expedited dispatch is available for urgent event deadlines.

What logo artwork format do you need?

High-resolution vector files (.AI, .EPS, .CDR, or vector .PDF) yield the crispest engraving and print quality. Our design studio can also assist in formatting your files.
